THE BRINNA EXPERIENCE

More than a restaurant. A place to feel at home.

Step in and settle down. Brinna Ventures blends the polish of a modern lounge with the soul of the Ghanaian table, where every soup is simmered the traditional way and every guest is welcomed like family.

On Sundays our kitchen turns to tradition with Omo Tuo, Tuo Zaafi and Konkonte, served the way they are meant to be shared. Curated hospitality, warm wood, clay pottery and woven textiles make every visit in Accra feel personal.

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Tap items in the menu, open your order, enter your name and phone, then send it to us on WhatsApp. Orders are taken on WhatsApp only.

Do you do delivery?

Send your order on WhatsApp and tell us where you are. We confirm delivery availability, fee and time in the chat.

Can you cater for large groups?

Absolutely. Send us the date and guest count in the form and we will come back with a quote.

When are the Sunday specials served?

Omo Tuo, Tuo Zaafi and Konkonte are served on Sundays only.
