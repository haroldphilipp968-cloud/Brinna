THE BRINNA EXPERIENCE

More than a restaurant. A place to feel at home.

Step in and settle down. Brinna blends the polish of a modern lounge with the soul of the Ghanaian table, where every soup is simmered the traditional way and every guest is welcomed like family.

On Sundays our kitchen turns to tradition with Omo Tuo, Tuo Zaafi and Konkonte, served the way they are meant to be shared. Curated hospitality, warm wood, clay pottery and woven textiles make every visit in Accra feel personal.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a table?+

Tap Book a Table, choose your date, time, guests and seating area, and we will confirm by phone.

Can I order for pickup or delivery?+

Yes. Add dishes to your order, choose Pickup or Delivery, and send the order to us on WhatsApp.

What are the Sunday specials?+

On Sundays we serve the traditional dishes: Omo Tuo, Tuo Zaafi and Konkonte, with a choice of local soups.

How do the soups work?+

Soups are the base. Pair your soup with the protein and accompaniment of your choice, such as fufu, banku, goat or fish.

Do you host events?+

Yes, please use the Events section above to send us the date and number of guests.
